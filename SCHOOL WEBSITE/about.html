Our History

Established in 1950 with a vision to transform education

TSGVHSS Payyoli was founded in 1950 with the mission to provide quality education to the youth of Payyoli and surrounding areas. Over the decades, we have grown from a small institution to one of the most respected higher secondary schools in Kerala.

Our journey began with just 50 students and 5 teachers. Today, we proudly serve over 2000 students with a dedicated faculty of 150 members, continuing our legacy of academic excellence and holistic development.
