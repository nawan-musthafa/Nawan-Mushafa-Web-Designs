Admission Process

Begin your educational journey with TSGVHSS Payyoli

Join Our Community of Learners

At TSGVHSS Payyoli, we welcome students who are eager to learn, grow, and contribute to our vibrant school community. Our admission process is designed to identify students who will thrive in our academic environment and benefit from our holistic educational approach.

We offer admission for classes 8th to 12th with streams in Science, Commerce, and Humanities at the higher secondary level.

Transparent Admission Process
Merit-Based Selection
Financial Aid Available

Admission Procedure

1

Inquiry & Application

Submit the online inquiry form or visit our campus to collect the admission form. Forms are available from January each year.

2

Document Submission

Submit the completed application form along with required documents including previous school records and certificates.

3

Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview with the admission committee along with their parents/guardians.

4

Admission Confirmation

Selected candidates will receive admission offers. Complete the admission formalities and fee payment to secure your seat.

Required Documents

For New Admissions

  • Birth Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate from previous school
  • Mark sheet of last examination passed
  • Aadhar Card of student
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs (4 copies)

For Parent/Guardian

  • Aadhar Card of parent/guardian
  • Recent passport size photograph (2 copies)
  • Address proof (Electricity bill/Ration card)
