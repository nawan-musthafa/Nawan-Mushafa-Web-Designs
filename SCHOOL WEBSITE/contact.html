Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school timings?

+

Regular school hours are from 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM, Monday to Friday. On Saturdays, school ends at 1:00 PM.

How can I schedule a campus visit?

+

You can schedule a campus visit by calling our office at +91 912871292 or by filling out the contact form on this page. We recommend scheduling visits on weekdays between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM.

Who should I contact for admission inquiries?

+

For admission-related queries, please contact our admission office at TSGVHSSpy@gmail.com or call +91 912871293 during office hours.

What documents are required for admission?

+

Required documents include: Birth certificate, previous school transcripts, transfer certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), and passport-sized photographs. Please visit our Admission page for complete details.
