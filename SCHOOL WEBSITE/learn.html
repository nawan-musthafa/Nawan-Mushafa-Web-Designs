Academics & Co-Curricular Life

Nurturing Excellence in Education and Beyond at TSGVHSS Payyoli

Our Academic Facilities

State-of-the-art infrastructure for comprehensive learning

Well-Stocked Library

Our library houses over 10,000 books, journals, and periodicals covering diverse subjects. With a dedicated reading area and digital resources, it serves as a knowledge hub for students and faculty alike. Regular book exhibitions and reading programs foster a love for literature and research.

10,000+ Books Digital Resources Reading Programs

Science Laboratories

Physics Lab

Equipped with modern apparatus for experiments in mechanics, optics, electricity, and thermodynamics. Students gain hands-on experience with practical applications of physics concepts.

Chemistry Lab

Fully equipped with safety measures for conducting experiments in organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry. Students learn proper laboratory techniques and safety protocols.

Biology Lab

Features microscopes, specimens, and models for studying botany, zoology, and human anatomy. Students develop scientific observation and analytical skills.

Clubs & Student Units

Developing leadership and character through diverse activities

NCC

National Cadet Corps

Our NCC unit instills discipline, leadership, and patriotism. Cadets participate in drills, camps, and social service activities, developing character and national spirit.

Drill Practice Annual Camps Social Service

SPC

Student Police Cadet

The SPC program develops responsible citizens with respect for law and order. Cadets learn traffic rules, first aid, and community policing principles.

Traffic Training First Aid Community Service

Scouts & Guides

Character Development

Promoting self-reliance, teamwork, and service through outdoor activities, camping, and community projects. Members learn survival skills and civic responsibility.

Camping Knots & Skills Community Projects

NSS

National Service Scheme

NSS volunteers engage in community service, environmental conservation, and health awareness programs. The motto "Not Me, But You" guides all activities.

Cleanliness Drives Health Camps Awareness Programs

Little Kites

Empowers young tech minds.

Little Kites is an IT club in Kerala that trains school students in coding, robotics, animation, and digital skills, helping them become creative and tech-savvy from a young age.

Techonolgy Coding Robotics

JRC

Junior Red Cross

JRC (Junior Red Cross) encourages students to care for others and serve the community. It promotes health, safety, and social responsibility through activities like first aid training, awareness programs, and community service.

Health & Hygiene First Aid Community Service
